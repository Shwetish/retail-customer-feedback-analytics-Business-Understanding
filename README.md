CRISP-DM Business Understanding framework for customer feedback analytics, NLP-driven sentiment extraction, and store operational optimization.

# 💬 Retail Customer Feedback Analytics & Store Profit Optimization

## 📌 1. Business Problem Statement
> *"Customer feedback is taken as an important aspect of the retail store. Considering customer feedback and making changes can increase the store profits and customer satisfaction."*

---

## 💡 2. Context & Problem Identification
* **The Issue:** Retail stores collect large volumes of customer feedback through surveys, online reviews, in-store feedback kiosks, and social media. However, this feedback often remains unorganized, unanalyzed, or slow to act upon, leading to unaddressed operational issues, lost sales, and customer churn.
* **The Solution:** Implement an automated Natural Language Processing (NLP) and customer feedback analytics pipeline to extract actionable sentiment, categorize operational issues (e.g., checkout speed, staff behavior, product quality), and translate customer insights into store-level operational improvements that boost profitability.

---

## 📊 3. Business Objectives & Constraints

### 🎯 Business Objectives
* **Maximize Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) & Net Promoter Score (NPS):** Continuously identify and resolve store friction points to improve overall customer experience.
* **Maximize Repeat Visit & Customer Retention Rates:** Turn negative experiences around by quickly addressing feedback and improving store operations.
* **Maximize Store Sales & Revenue:** Implement customer-driven store layout, product availability, and service changes that directly drive basket size and sales conversion.

### ⚠️ Constraints & Limitations
* **Minimize Feedback Action Latency:** Process and route negative feedback alerts to store managers rapidly to address urgent service failures.
* **Minimize Implementation & Operational Costs:** Ensure store operational adjustments recommended by the feedback model are cost-effective and yield a positive ROI.

---

## 👥 4. Stakeholder Expectations
* **Store Managers & Regional Directors:** Require daily/weekly feedback dashboards highlighting top store operational pain points and suggested corrective actions.
* **Customer Service & Operations Teams:** Expect automated categorizations (e.g., pricing complaints vs. long queue times) to assign tasks to responsible teams efficiently.
* **Executive Leadership:** Require measurable proof linking customer feedback resolution to store profitability and footfall metrics.

---

## ⚙️ 5. Feasibility Assessment
* **Technical Feasibility:** High. Sentiment classification, topic modeling (LDA/BERTopic), and text mining techniques are well-established for structured and unstructured customer review datasets.
* **Data Feasibility:** High. Retailers collect feedback across digital survey forms, Google/Yelp reviews, loyalty app ratings, point-of-sale receipt codes, and customer support tickets.

---

## 📈 6. Success Criteria

| Category | Success Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Business Success** | • 15%+ increase in store CSAT / NPS scores within 6 months.<br>• 10% lift in repeat customer visits across optimized store locations. |
| **ML Success** | • Sentiment Classification F1-Score > 0.88.<br>• Topic Modeling Accuracy > 85% on categorizing operational complaint drivers. |

---

## 🚀 7. Business Impact
Closing the feedback loop with predictive data analytics enables retail store managers to make targeted, evidence-based operational changes, building long-term customer loyalty and driving higher store margins.
